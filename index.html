Meet the Founder
Maxwell Klean
Founder — Klean Studios LLC & L.F.T.5 Series

Maxwell is the founder of Klean Studios LLC and the originator of the Live From The 5ive event series. Known for his expert-level audio engineering and his executive production work on The Biscuits and Gravy Show, Maxwell has spent years building a platform that empowers local artists.

Since 2022, he has worked with more than 300 artists across both in-studio recordings and live performance productions — bringing an unmatched level of craft and community focus to every project.
